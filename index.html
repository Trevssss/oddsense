ONE CLEARER VIEW

Everything you need to read the market.

OddSense is an analysis workspace, not a bookmaker. It helps organize fixture information and compare available prices before you make your own decision.

◈

01 · FIXTURES

Follow the schedule

Browse live and upcoming events across the sports supported by your configured data products. Move between today and the next two weeks.

⌁

02 · ODDS CONTEXT

Compare what prices imply

When current odds are available, OddSense calculates a bookmaker-implied probability from listed outcomes. That reflects a market price—not a guaranteed result or independent prediction.

✦

03 · MATCH ANALYSIS

Ask focused questions

Review the data actually supplied for a fixture. Missing form, injury or lineup coverage is called out rather than filled in with invented statistics.

▤

04 · BETSLIP WORKSPACE

Prepare a portable slip

Combine selected markets, calculate total odds and potential payout, then copy or share a formatted list for manual entry in your chosen app.

◎

05 · SPORTS COVERAGE

See configured sports

The dashboard lists the sports enabled by your provider configuration. Fixture availability still depends on your API plan, leagues, odds coverage and selected dates.

The dashboard reports when the live feed is unavailable. It never silently substitutes made-up sample fixtures or generates fake bookmaker booking codes.

DATA & ACCURACY

Clear about what OddSense can—and can’t—know.

Sports outcomes are uncertain. No AI or statistics can make predictions right every time. Odds-derived percentages describe the market price; they do not prove that an outcome is likely to happen.

✓

Live football fixtures require a GOAL API key. The key is read by the local server and is not included in browser code.

✓

Sport coverage depends on your API products and plan. Additional sports need an available product endpoint and compatible adapter.

✓

Odds are not performance statistics. Form, head-to-head, injuries and lineups only appear when a connected source actually provides them.

✓

Final-result feedback is score-based. OddSense only marks a selection won or lost after a provider reports a verified final score.

YOUR PICKS, YOUR DECISION

A clearer slip.
No fake booking codes.

SportyBet, Bet9ja and MSport do not share one universal booking-code format. OddSense prepares a manual-entry summary; it does not place bets or claim that a generated code will work.
